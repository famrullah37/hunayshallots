Fried shallots are now a standard topping far beyond Southeast Asia. Ramen shops in Japan, Korean kitchens, Asian grocery chains in the US, Canada and Australia, and food manufacturers all buy them in bulk.

Indonesia is one of the best places to source them. This guide explains why, and walks you through the import process from first inquiry to delivery.

## Why Source Fried Shallots from Indonesia?

**Shallots are a core crop.** Indonesia grows shallots on a large scale, and *bawang goreng* (fried shallots) is part of everyday cooking. Producers have deep experience with slicing, frying and drying.

**Distinct shallot varieties.** Varieties like **biru lancor** from Probolinggo, East Java, are small and dense with low water content. They fry evenly, turn golden instead of dark, and stay crisp longer than larger, wetter shallots.

**No batter needed.** Traditional Indonesian fried shallots are just shallots, oil and salt. Many cheaper products on the world market are coated with flour or starch to add weight. Buyers who want a clean label prefer the pure version.

**Halal supply.** Indonesia has a well-established halal certification system (Halal MUI), which matters for buyers in Asia, the Middle East, and Muslim communities worldwide.

## Who Buys Imported Fried Shallots?

- **Importers and distributors** supplying Asian grocery stores
- **Restaurant groups** serving ramen, pho, laksa, nasi goreng and rice bowls
- **Food manufacturers** using fried shallots in instant noodles, seasonings, sauces, snacks and ready meals
- **Private label brands** that want their own packaging and label

## The Import Process, Step by Step

### 1. Send an inquiry

Share your company name, destination country, the product you need (fried shallots, fried garlic or both), and your expected monthly volume. This lets the supplier quote the right packaging and price.

### 2. Test samples

Always test samples before ordering. Check color, crispness, oil content and taste. If you are a manufacturer, test them inside your own product, not only straight from the bag.

### 3. Agree on specifications

Confirm in writing:

- Ingredients (ideally shallots, oil, salt only)
- Cut size and color range
- Packaging type and size (retail pouch, jar, or bulk bags in cartons)
- Shelf life in that packaging
- Labeling requirements for your country

### 4. Confirm commercial terms

Agree on **minimum order quantity (MOQ)**, price, **Incoterms** (such as FOB or CIF), payment terms and lead time. Small first orders are common while you test the market.

### 5. Prepare documents

Ask your supplier which documents they can provide for your shipment. Importers commonly request:

- Commercial invoice and packing list
- Certificate of origin
- Halal certificate
- Product specification sheet
- Certificates such as HACCP or ISO 9001

Requirements differ by country, so check with your customs broker or food authority before the first shipment. They can also confirm the correct HS code for your product.

### 6. Ship and receive

Fried shallots are light and shelf-stable, so they ship well by sea or air without refrigeration. The main risk is moisture.

## Keeping Fried Shallots Crisp During Shipping

Moisture is the biggest enemy of fried shallots. On a long sea journey, temperature changes can cause condensation inside containers. To protect quality:

- Use **vacuum-sealed or nitrogen-flushed packs** with a good moisture barrier.
- Pack in **strong cartons** to prevent crushing.
- Store in a **cool, dry warehouse** after arrival — never in a cold room, where humidity makes them soft.
- Rotate stock **first in, first out**.

Well-packed fried shallots typically keep 6–12 months unopened.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Choosing on price alone.** Cheap fried shallots often contain flour or are fried in old oil, which shortens shelf life and leaves a bitter taste.
- **Skipping samples.** Photos do not show crispness or flavor.
- **Not checking certificates.** Ask for copies, and make sure they are current.
- **Ignoring packaging.** The same product can last weeks or months depending on the pack.
