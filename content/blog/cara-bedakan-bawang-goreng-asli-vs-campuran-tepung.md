Pernahkah Anda membeli bawang goreng yang terasa beda — kurang harum, agak keras, dan teksturnya seperti kerupuk? Kemungkinan besar, bawang goreng tersebut **dicampur dengan tepung**.

## 5 Cara Mudah Membedakan

### 1. Perhatikan Bentuk
- **Asli:** Irisan tipis melengkung, ukuran tidak seragam, permukaan natural
- **Campuran tepung:** Bulat-bulat seperti kerupuk kecil, ukuran seragam, permukaan halus

### 2. Uji Aroma
- **Asli:** Aroma bawang kuat dan tajam, tercium dari jarak 30 cm
- **Campuran:** Aroma tipis, ada bau tepung atau minyak yang dominan

### 3. Remas Teksturnya
- **Asli:** Langsung hancur menjadi remahan halus, leleh di mulut
- **Campuran:** Lebih keras, terasa seperti kerupuk, ada rasa tepung

### 4. Cek Label
Jika ada "tepung beras", "tepung tapioka", atau "pati" dalam komposisi bahan — itu campuran.

### 5. Uji Air (Paling Definitif)
Masukkan 5–10 butir ke segelas air:
- **Asli:** Mengapung, air tetap jernih
- **Campuran:** Sebagian tenggelam, air keruh, ada endapan putih di dasar

Have you ever bought fried onions that tasted different — less fragrant, slightly hard, and with a texture like crackers? Chances are, those fried onions were **mixed with flour**.

## 5 Easy Ways to Tell the Difference

### 1. Check the Shape
- **Real:** Thin curved slices, non-uniform size, natural surface texture
- **Flour mixture:** Round like small crackers, uniform size, smooth surface

### 2. Smell the Aroma
- **Real:** Strong, sharp onion aroma detectable from 30 cm away
- **Mixture:** Faint aroma, flour or oil smell dominant

### 3. Squeeze the Texture
- **Real:** Crumbles immediately into fine pieces, melts in your mouth
- **Mixture:** Harder, feels like crackers, floury aftertaste

### 4. Check the Label
If you see "rice flour," "tapioca starch," or "starch" in the ingredients list — it's a mixture.

### 5. The Water Test (Most Definitive)
Drop 5–10 pieces into a glass of water:
- **Real:** Floats on surface, water stays clear
- **Mixture:** Some sink, water turns cloudy, white sediment at the bottom
