Behind every crispy, fragrant bite of Hunay fried shallots lies one special raw material: **biru lancor shallots from Probolinggo**.

Not all shallots can produce quality fried shallots. Choosing the right variety is the first — and most important — step in premium fried shallot production.

## What Are Biru Lancor Shallots?

Biru lancor is a local shallot variety developed and cultivated for generations by farmers in Probolinggo, East Java, Indonesia. The name "biru lancor" comes from its distinctive characteristics: a reddish bulb with a purplish-blue tint, and an elongated, slender shape (*lancor* means slender/elongated in Javanese).

## Why Biru Lancor Is Superior for Frying

### 1. Very Low Water Content

Its low water content means:
- Faster and more even frying
- Crispier, drier final result
- Stays crunchy longer after frying
- Naturally longer shelf life

### 2. Stronger and More Distinctive Aroma

Biru lancor contains higher sulfur compounds than other varieties, producing the characteristic strong shallot aroma.

### 3. Naturally Savory-Sweet Flavor

The combination of natural sugars and sulfur compounds creates a complex flavor profile: savory with a hint of sweetness at the finish.

### 4. Uniform Size

Relatively small and uniform bulbs make it easy to slice them thinly and consistently, ensuring even cooking.

## Why Does It Only Grow Best in Probolinggo?

- **Volcanic soil** from Mount Bromo, rich in minerals
- **Dry climate** with low humidity ideal for shallot cultivation
- **High sunlight intensity** that helps form aromatic compounds
- **Right altitude** (0–500 m above sea level)

## Biru Lancor vs Other Varieties

| Aspect | Biru Lancor Probolinggo | Regular Shallots |
|---|---|---|
| Water content | Low | Medium–High |
| Aroma | Very strong | Moderate |
| Flavor after frying | Savory-sweet | Tends to be sharp |
| Post-frying crispiness | Excellent | Moderate |
| Suitable for frying? | Highly ideal | Less optimal |

This is why Hunay does not use other varieties, even if they are cheaper. Raw material quality is a foundation that cannot be compromised.
