## Bahan-Bahan (4 porsi)

**Ayam Crispy:** 4 potong ayam, 200g tepung terigu, 50g tepung beras, 1 butir telur, bumbu (bawang putih bubuk, garam, merica).

**Sambal Bawang:** 15 cabai rawit merah, 5 cabai merah keriting, 8 siung bawang merah, 5 siung bawang putih, garam, gula, 3 sdm minyak, **2 sdm bawang goreng renyah**.

## Cara Membuat

**Langkah 1 — Marinasi:** Lumuri ayam dengan bumbu, diamkan 30 menit di kulkas.

**Langkah 2 — Lapisan Crispy:** Campurkan tepung kering. Celupkan ayam ke telur, gulingkan ke tepung. Diamkan 10 menit sebelum digoreng.

**Langkah 3 — Goreng:** Api sedang-besar, 8–10 menit per sisi hingga coklat keemasan.

**Langkah 4 — Sambal:** Ulek kasar cabai dan bawang. Tumis hingga matang dan harum (8–10 menit). Tambahkan garam dan gula. Matikan api, masukkan **bawang goreng renyah** — ini rahasianya!

**Langkah 5 — Sajikan:** Letakkan ayam di cobek, taruh sambal di atasnya, geprek. Sajikan dengan nasi dan lalapan.

---

## Tips
- Untuk keluarga dengan anak-anak, kurangi cabai rawit
- Sambal matang tahan **5 hari** di kulkas
- **Variasi:** Geprek Keju, Geprek Mozzarella, Geprek Saus Mentai

## Ingredients (4 servings)

**Crispy Chicken:** 4 chicken pieces, 200g all-purpose flour, 50g rice flour, 1 egg, seasonings (garlic powder, salt, white pepper).

**Shallot Chili Sauce:** 15 bird's eye chilies, 5 curly red chilies, 8 shallots, 5 garlic cloves, salt, sugar, 3 tbsp oil, **2 tbsp crispy fried shallots**.

## Method

**Step 1 — Marinate:** Season the chicken with spices and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

**Step 2 — Crispy Coating:** Mix the dry flour ingredients. Dip chicken in egg, coat in flour mixture. Rest 10 minutes before frying.

**Step 3 — Fry:** Medium-high heat, 8–10 minutes per side until golden brown.

**Step 4 — Sambal:** Coarsely pound chilies and shallots. Sauté until fully cooked and fragrant (8–10 minutes). Add salt and sugar. Turn off heat, stir in **crispy fried shallots** — this is the secret!

**Step 5 — Serve:** Place chicken on a mortar or plate, add sambal on top, smash together. Serve with rice and fresh vegetables.

---

## Tips
- For families with children, reduce the bird's eye chilies
- Cooked sambal keeps for **5 days** refrigerated
- **Variations:** Cheese Geprek, Mozzarella Geprek, Mentai Sauce Geprek
