Ciri khas Soto Lamongan: **kuah kuning bening** (bukan santan), koya (kerupuk udang + bawang putih goreng), dan **bawang goreng berlimpah** di atasnya.

## Bahan-Bahan (6 porsi)

**Bahan Utama:** 1 ekor ayam kampung, 2 liter air, serai, daun salam, daun jeruk, lengkuas.

**Bumbu Halus:** 8 siung bawang merah, 5 siung bawang putih, 4 cm kunyit (bakar), 3 cm jahe, 3 butir kemiri (sangrai), 1 sdt ketumbar, ½ sdt jinten.

**Pelengkap:** Bawang goreng renyah, koya, tauge, soun, telur rebus, seledri, daun bawang, jeruk nipis.

## Cara Membuat

**Langkah 1:** Rebus ayam dengan bumbu aromatik selama 45 menit hingga empuk. Saring kaldu.

**Langkah 2:** Haluskan dan tumis bumbu halus hingga **matang sempurna** (penting — bumbu mentah berbau langu). Masukkan ke kaldu.

**Langkah 3:** Panaskan kembali kaldu. Suwir ayam. Buat koya: haluskan kerupuk udang + bawang putih goreng 1:1.

**Langkah 4 — Sajikan:** Susun soun, tauge, suwiran ayam, telur rebus. Tuang kuah panas. Taburi **bawang goreng berlimpah**, koya, seledri, perasan jeruk nipis.

---

## Tips Penting

**Soal Bawang Goreng:** Gunakan bawang goreng premium seperti Bawang Goreng Hunay — Biru Lancor dengan kadar air rendah **tidak langsung melempem** terkena uap panas kuah.

**Soal Kunyit:** Bakar sebelum dihaluskan untuk aroma lebih intens dan warna lebih cantik.

Lamongan chicken soup is characterized by a **clear yellow broth** (not coconut milk), koya (shrimp crackers + fried garlic), and **generous fried shallots** on top.

## Ingredients (6 servings)

**Main:** 1 free-range chicken, 2 liters water, lemongrass, bay leaves, kaffir lime leaves, galangal.

**Spice Paste:** 8 shallots, 5 garlic cloves, 4 cm turmeric (toasted), 3 cm ginger, 3 candlenuts (toasted), 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp cumin.

**Garnishes:** Crispy fried shallots, koya, bean sprouts, glass noodles, boiled eggs, celery, spring onion, lime.

## Method

**Step 1:** Simmer chicken with aromatic ingredients for 45 minutes until tender. Strain the broth.

**Step 2:** Grind and sauté the spice paste until **fully cooked and fragrant** (important — undercooked spices smell raw and grassy). Add to broth.

**Step 3:** Reheat the spiced broth. Shred the chicken. Make koya: blend shrimp crackers + fried garlic 1:1.

**Step 4 — Serve:** Layer glass noodles, bean sprouts, shredded chicken, boiled eggs in a bowl. Ladle hot broth over. Top generously with **crispy fried shallots**, koya, celery, and a squeeze of lime.

---

## Key Tips

**On Fried Shallots:** Use premium fried shallots like Hunay — Biru Lancor's low water content means they **won't immediately go soft** from the hot steam of the broth.

**On Turmeric:** Toast it before grinding for a more intense aroma and a more beautiful color.
