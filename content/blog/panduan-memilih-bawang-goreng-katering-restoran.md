Bagi pelaku usaha katering dan restoran, **pilihan bawang goreng yang salah bisa berdampak langsung ke reputasi bisnis Anda**. Berikut 7 faktor penting yang harus dipertimbangkan.

## 1. Konsistensi Kualitas Antar Batch
Minta sample dari 2–3 batch berbeda. Tanyakan sistem QC supplier secara detail.

## 2. Sertifikasi Resmi
- **Halal MUI** — wajib untuk konsumen Muslim
- **BPOM/PIRT** — keamanan pangan resmi
- ISO 9001, HACCP, GMP (nilai tambah)

## 3. Stabilitas Pasokan
Pastikan kapasitas produksi jauh melebihi kebutuhan rutin Anda dan ada stok buffer.

## 4. Pilihan Kemasan
- **Kiloan (1–10 kg):** lebih hemat, cocok katering volume tinggi
- **Satuan (100–200 gr):** lebih mudah dikontrol, cocok restoran

## 5. Transparansi Harga
Hitung total biaya: harga beli + ongkir + susut akibat produk melempem.

## 6. Responsivitas Supplier
Uji: kirim pertanyaan di luar jam kerja, tanyakan hal teknis, coba sampaikan complaint kecil.

## 7. Komitmen Jangka Panjang
Tanyakan: program loyalitas, skema pembayaran, fleksibilitas repeat order.

For catering and restaurant businesses, **choosing the wrong fried onion supplier can directly impact your business reputation**. Here are 7 key factors to consider.

## 1. Batch-to-Batch Quality Consistency
Request samples from 2–3 different production batches. Ask the supplier to explain their QC system in detail.

## 2. Official Certifications
- **Halal MUI** — mandatory for Muslim consumers
- **BPOM/PIRT** — official food safety clearance
- ISO 9001, HACCP, GMP (added value)

## 3. Supply Stability
Ensure production capacity far exceeds your routine needs and that buffer stock is maintained.

## 4. Packaging Options
- **Bulk (1–10 kg):** more cost-efficient, suitable for high-volume catering
- **Retail units (100–200 gr):** easier quality control, suitable for restaurants

## 5. Price Transparency
Calculate total cost: purchase price + shipping + waste from product going soft.

## 6. Supplier Responsiveness
Test: send a question outside business hours, ask technical questions, try a small complaint.

## 7. Long-Term Commitment
Ask about: loyalty programs, payment schemes, repeat order flexibility.
