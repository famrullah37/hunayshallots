Fried shallots are a small ingredient with a big effect. One spoon adds crunch, a sweet roasted aroma and a little saltiness. That is why they show up in so many Asian bowls — not only Indonesian food.

Here is how to use them in four popular dishes.

## The Basic Rule: Add Them Last

Fried shallots go soft in hot liquid within a few minutes. For the best texture:

- Add them **right before serving**, on top of the bowl.
- Keep some on the table so people can add more.
- Use about **1 tablespoon per bowl**.

## Ramen

Ramen already has rich broth and fatty toppings. Fried shallots add a light, crunchy contrast.

- **Best with:** shoyu, miso and tonkotsu ramen.
- **How:** sprinkle over the chashu and egg, next to green onion.
- **Tip:** mix a pinch into the tare or chili oil for extra aroma.

## Pho

In Vietnam, fried shallots (*hành phi*) are a common pho topping, especially for chicken pho.

- **Best with:** pho gà (chicken) and pho bò (beef).
- **How:** add with the fresh herbs — basil, cilantro and lime.
- **Tip:** fried shallots balance the clean, light broth with a little sweetness.

## Laksa

Laksa is a creamy, spicy coconut noodle soup from Malaysia and Singapore.

- **Best with:** curry laksa and Singapore laksa.
- **How:** sprinkle on top with bean sprouts, boiled egg and sambal.
- **Tip:** the sweetness of shallots softens the heat of the chili.

## Bibimbap

Fried shallots are not traditional in Korean bibimbap, but they work very well as a modern twist.

- **Best with:** bibimbap with beef or tofu, and gochujang sauce.
- **How:** add on top just before mixing, so some pieces stay crunchy.
- **Tip:** try fried garlic too — it pairs well with sesame oil.

## Other Bowls to Try

- **Chicken rice** (Hainanese style)
- **Congee** or rice porridge
- **Udon** and **soba**
- **Poke bowls**
- **Instant noodles** — the easiest upgrade at home

## Fried Shallots or Fried Garlic?

Both are good. Choose by flavor:

- **Fried shallots:** sweet, mild, fragrant — good for most soups.
- **Fried garlic:** stronger and more savory — good for beef, rice and stir-fries.

Many cooks use both together.

## Storage Tip

Keep the jar closed between uses and store it away from the stove. Steam from cooking makes fried shallots soft. If they lose crispness, warm them for 2–3 minutes in a dry pan.

## Quick Summary

- Add fried shallots at the end, about 1 tablespoon per bowl.
- They fit ramen, pho, laksa, bibimbap and many other bowls.
- Use fried garlic for a stronger, more savory flavor.
