Fried shallots are more than just a garnish — they are a flavor enhancer that elevates ordinary dishes into something extraordinary.

## 1. Nasi Goreng Kampung (Village Fried Rice)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 plates cold cooked rice
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 3 red chilies, sliced
- 2 eggs
- 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce (*kecap manis*)
- 1 tsp salt
- **Hunay fried shallots** generously for topping

**Method:** Sauté garlic, add chilies and eggs, add rice and stir-fry over high heat. Season with kecap manis and salt. Top with **Hunay fried shallots**.

## 2. Soto Ayam Bening (Clear Chicken Soup)

**Broth ingredients:** Whole chicken, lemongrass, bay leaves, ginger, spice paste of shallots, garlic, turmeric.

**Garnishes:** Bean sprouts, glass noodles, boiled eggs, tomato, **generous Hunay fried shallots**, spring onion, lime wedges.

---

## 3. Bubur Ayam (Chicken Rice Porridge)

Cook rice in chicken broth until it breaks down into thick porridge. Serve with shredded chicken, ginger, spring onion, fried dough sticks, and **Hunay fried shallots** — the key ingredient!

---

## 4. Mie Ayam Bakso (Chicken Noodle with Meatballs)

Toss egg noodles with shallot oil. Serve with minced chicken topping, meatballs, and broth. **Scatter Hunay fried shallots** generously on top before serving.

---

## 5. Tempe Orek Pedas Manis (Sweet and Spicy Tempeh)

Sauté shallots and garlic, add chilies and crispy fried tempeh, season with kecap manis and palm sugar. Serve topped with **crispy Hunay fried shallots**.
